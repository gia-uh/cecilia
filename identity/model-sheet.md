# Cecilia — Model Sheet

The rigid character canon. This document is included verbatim in mosaico render prompts as hard rules. Every render of Cecilia must satisfy every rule below or it is not Cecilia.

## Identity

- Cuban woman, mulata (mixed African and European heritage), Habanera.
- Mid-twenties (24–28), young adult, never adolescent and never older.
- Contemporary — not 19th-century period costume, not retro-styling. (Cecilia Valdés is the literary subtext, not the visual reference.)

## Face

- Skin: warm caramel-to-copper range. Avoid pale, avoid very dark, avoid yellow undertones.
- Hair: dark brown to near-black, wavy / softly curly (not straight, not tight curls), mid-length (just past the shoulders), often partially up so the face is unobstructed.
- Eyebrows: full, dark, well-defined, naturally shaped.
- Eyes: deep brown, large, slightly almond.
- Nose: rounded, soft.
- Lips: full, neutral natural color (no bright lipstick).
- Cheekbones: prominent.

## Gaze and posture

- **Aspirational upward gaze** — looking forward and slightly up, eyes open and confident. This is the diagnostic signature pose. Never downcast, never coquettish, never sideways-glancing.
- Posture: upright, shoulders back, neck long. Confident and at ease.
- Expression: composed, slightly smiling at most. Never broad-smiling, never pouting, never sultry.

## Earrings — diagnostic detail

- **Long teardrop white earrings.** Solid white, narrow at the top, rounded at the bottom, ~5cm long, hanging straight.
- White is the only "non-skin, non-hair" color on her body. The earrings are diagnostic — a render without them is not Cecilia.

## Wardrobe — the canon

- **Female guayabera dress.** White or cream colored. Contemporary cut (not period). Embroidered front panels are acceptable but optional. The garment must be recognisably a guayabera (vertical pleats / alforzas, four pockets or pocket details, button placket).
- Alternative: guayabera blouse + complementary skirt (skirt color: cream, white, or warm earth tone — never bright/saturated).
- No jewelry beyond the white teardrop earrings (no necklaces, no bracelets, no rings).
- No headwear.
- No glasses.

## Rendering style — hero register

- Illustrated, vector / hand-drawn aesthetic. **Not photorealistic.**
- Limited palette: warm browns, deep near-black, crisp white. See [`palette.yml`](palette.yml).
- **Negative-space white planes** carve the face where light hits (forehead, cheekbone, jaw, side of neck). White is light, not paint.
- Hard edges, no gradients, no soft brushstrokes, no painterly textures.
- Gestural white sweeps may appear in the background composition, suggesting architecture without rendering it.
- Background: cool neutral gray ground (#b0b5b9 — the canonical bust's ground, sampled). No environmental scenery, no objects, no other characters.

## Hard prohibitions

These violations make a render not-Cecilia:

- ❌ Pale or very dark skin (must be warm caramel-to-copper).
- ❌ Period costume / 19th-century styling.
- ❌ Headwear (turban, scarf, hat, flower in hair).
- ❌ Bright lipstick or heavy makeup.
- ❌ Multiple jewelry pieces (only the white teardrop earrings).
- ❌ Photographic realism.
- ❌ Soft / gradient / painterly rendering.
- ❌ Downcast or coquettish gaze.
- ❌ Background scenery, objects, or other characters in canonical hero renders.
- ❌ Wardrobe other than guayabera (in canonical register; v2 secondary registers may relax this with explicit override).
